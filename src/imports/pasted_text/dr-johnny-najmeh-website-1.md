
Design a modern, elegant, fully responsive website for Zahnarzt  Johnny Najmeh, a dental practice in Freiburg, Germany. The website must reflect trust, comfort, modern technology, and ease of booking. It should be visually appealing, clean, and professional – suitable for a premium healthcare provider.

Brand / Tone
Elegant, calm, medical yet warm

Colors: White, soft teal/blue (trust), gold or warm accent (comfort), dark gray for text

Typography: Clean sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Montserrat) for body, refined serif (e.g., Playfair Display) for headings

Imagery: High-quality dental clinic photos, smiling patients, modern equipment, friendly doctor
